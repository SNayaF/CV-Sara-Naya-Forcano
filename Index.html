About me

I’m Sara Naya Forcano, PhD in Neurobiology with a background in Biotechnology and Neuroscience, currently working as a Field Application Specialist in the IVD field.

After several years in academic research, I made the transition into industry, where I found a role that combines my scientific background with technical support, problem-solving, communication and direct interaction with healthcare professionals. I particularly enjoy the dynamic nature of my work, the opportunity to solve complex problems and the continuous learning that comes with working in diagnostics.

My research background has given me strong analytical skills, scientific rigor and the ability to manage complex projects from experimental design to data analysis and scientific communication. Working in multidisciplinary environments has also strengthened my adaptability, organisation, autonomy and ability to communicate with people from different professional backgrounds.

I’m particularly motivated by opportunities where I can combine scientific knowledge with practical problem-solving and make a real impact. While I’m very happy developing my career as a Field Application Specialist, I’m also open to exploring new areas where I can transfer my experience, learn from new perspectives and continue growing professionally.

I’m curious, adaptable and a fast learner, and I enjoy working in challenging environments, including situations that require managing multiple priorities, working under pressure and finding effective solutions.
